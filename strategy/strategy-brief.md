# Strategy Brief - F1 Pit Wall Compose Mode

## Audience

AI-native builders, product engineers, technical founders, and solution architects building SaaS products with Cursor, Claude Code, Lovable, or Replit.

## Pain

Builders can generate apps and dashboards quickly, but analytics becomes fragile when they need reusable, trusted, embedded, filterable, explainable product analytics.

## Prototype

F1 Pit Wall is a familiar, high-signal telemetry use case that shows how raw data becomes an embedded decision experience.

## PLG CTA

Clone the repo, run local mode, connect Sisense Trial, generate data model, switch to Sisense Mode, and build your own embedded analytics feature.
